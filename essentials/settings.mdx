This quickstart takes you from a new weya account to a live campaign using the dashboard. You build an agent, connect it to a channel, launch a campaign and check its results. If you would rather drive campaigns from your own system, follow the [API quickstart](/get-started/api-quickstart) once you have an agent and a phone number.

<Info>
  Choose your channel in step 2. Each later step has a tab per channel, so you only follow the instructions for the agent you are building.
</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    1. Open the [weya dashboard](https://nexus.weya.ai/signup) and sign up.
    2. Verify your email address.
    3. Complete your workspace setup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an agent type">
    weya has four agent types. Pick the one that matches what you want to automate.

    | Agent type | Use it for |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Phone agent** | One-to-one inbound or outbound calls: sales, support, collections, IVR replacement |
    | **Voice blaster** | High-volume outbound voice broadcasts, either **Static** (the same message for everyone) or **Dynamic** (an AI-generated message personalised per contact) |
    | **Video agent** | Face-to-face conversations with an AI avatar. Currently **inbound only**: people join through a link |
    | **WhatsApp agent** | Conversations, reminders, order updates and support on WhatsApp |

    Go to **Agents**, click **Create an Agent** and select the agent type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the agent">
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Phone agent">
        1. Choose the agent category: **Single-Prompt Agent**, or **Multi-Prompt Agent** for complex flows.
        2. Select the call type: **Outbound** or **Inbound**.
        3. Click **Choose Template** and continue.
        4. Edit the **Prompt** and **System Message**.
        5. Set the voice, language and interruption sensitivity.
        6. Click **Save**.

        For every setting in detail, see [Build a voice agent](/single-prompt/overview).
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Voice blaster">
        1. Select the blaster type:
           - **Static**: upload or record one message that plays the same way for every contact.
           - **Dynamic**: write a **Prompt** and **System Message**, and the AI generates a personalised message for each contact, for example with their name, order details or due date.
        2. Set the voice and language. For a Dynamic blaster, also set how it responds when the person interrupts.
        3. Click **Save**.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Video agent">
        1. Choose an **Avatar**, or upload your own, and select a voice that matches it.
        2. Click **Choose Template** and continue.
        3. Edit the **Prompt** and **System Message**. They control what the avatar says and how it responds.
        4. Click **Save**.

        See [Create a video agent](/video-agents/create-video-agent) for the full setup.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="WhatsApp agent">
        1. Choose the agent category: **Single-Prompt Agent** or **Flow-Based Agent**.
        2. Select the conversation type: **Outbound Broadcast** or **Inbound Support**.
        3. Click **Choose Template** and continue.
        4. Edit the **Prompt**, **System Message** and any quick reply buttons or menus.
        5. Click **Save**.

        See [Create a WhatsApp agent](/Whatsapp/create-whatsapp-agent) for the full setup.
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>

    <Tip>
      Before you connect a channel, give the agent the information it needs: attach a [knowledge base](/knowledgebase/knowledgebase) and define [post-call metrics](/single-prompt/step4) for the results you want back from every call.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a channel">
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Phone agent or voice blaster">
        Voice blasters use the same phone setup as phone agents.

        1. Go to **Phone Numbers**.
        2. Click **Add Number**, then **Add Phone Number**.
        3. Choose your telephony provider, enter its credentials and click **Save**.
        4. Click **Assign Outbound Agent**, select your agent and click **Connect Agent**.

        See [Connect a phone number](/phonenumbers/addphonenumber) for the credentials each provider needs.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Video agent">
        There is nothing to connect. weya creates the video link when you create the campaign in the next step.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="WhatsApp agent">
        1. Go to **WhatsApp** and add your number through the WhatsApp Business API (the number must be verified by Meta).
        2. Enter your Business ID and credentials, then click **Save**.
        3. Click **Assign WhatsApp Agent**, select your agent and click **Connect Agent**.

        See [Connect a WhatsApp number](/Whatsapp/connect-whatsapp-number) for the requirements.
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch a campaign">
    1. Go to **Campaigns**, click **Create Campaign** and choose your channel.
    2. Add your contacts: upload a CSV file or connect a CRM.
    3. For voice campaigns, set the calling numbers, calling days and hours, timezone and retry rules.
    4. Create the campaign.

    For a video campaign, weya creates the join link at this step. Copy it and share it with your contacts.

    See [Create a campaign](/campaigns/createcampaign) for every field, including the CSV format.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the results">
    Open the campaign to follow it while it runs: how many calls were picked up and completed, each call's status, duration and retry count, and the transcript analysis for each conversation.

    See [Campaign analytics](/campaigns/analytics) for what each view shows.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  Your agent is now live on its channel. To send each result to your own system automatically, set up a [call result webhook](/integration/webhooks) or a [CRM integration](/integration/crmintegration).
</Check>
