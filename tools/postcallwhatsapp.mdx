The WhatsApp notifier sends a WhatsApp template message automatically when a weya voice call ends, for example a payment link, a confirmation or a summary of what was agreed.

### Add a WhatsApp number

If you haven't added a WhatsApp number yet:

1. Go to **Phone Numbers** and click **Add WhatsApp Number**.
2. Enter the WhatsApp phone number and the auth token from our WhatsApp service provider, **AI Sensy**.

### Turn on the notifier

1. Open the agent's **Tools & Knowledge Base** settings.
2. Select **Whatsapp Notifier**. Your WhatsApp number now appears in the dropdown.

---

### Select Number & Template

1. Open the **WhatsApp Number** dropdown and select the number you just added.  
2. Choose the **template** you want to send automatically after the call ends.  

---

### Add Variables

1. Fill in each of the template's variables, for example the customer's first name.
2. Click **Save**.

---

<Tip>
  Make sure the WhatsApp number is verified with AI Sensy before you select it. Once saved, the message is sent automatically after each call ends.
</Tip>
