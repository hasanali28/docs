The first message and the system prompt decide how your weya voice agent sounds and behaves on every call. The first message is the opening line the agent speaks; the system prompt tells it who it is, what it is trying to achieve and how to speak.

## First message

The first line the agent speaks when a call connects. Keep it short, say who is calling and why, and match your brand's tone.

```text
Hi, this is Alex from TechSolutions customer support. How can I help you today?
```

For outbound calls, use your contact list's variables so the opening is personal, for example the customer's name.

## System prompt

The system prompt defines the agent's role, goal, tone and limits. A good prompt covers:

- **Who the agent is** and who it works for.
- **The goal of the call**, ideally the same outcome your post-call metrics measure.
- **How it should speak**: tone, pace, formality.
- **What it must not do**, and when to hand over to a person.

```text Example system prompt
You are Alex, a customer service voice assistant for TechSolutions. Your purpose is to help
customers resolve issues with their products, answer questions about services and make sure
they leave satisfied.

- Sound friendly, patient and knowledgeable, never condescending.
- Use a conversational tone with natural speech patterns.
- Speak with confidence, and say so when you don't know something.
- Show genuine concern for the customer's issue.
```

<Tip>
  Phone conversations are spoken, not read. Ask the agent for short sentences and one question at a time, and avoid lists, symbols and abbreviations it would have to read aloud.
</Tip>

## Voice

1. Open the **Voice Selection** dropdown.
2. Preview the voices and choose one.

Test the voice together with your first message. Check that it sounds natural and clear in the agent's language, and that it fits the people you are calling.
