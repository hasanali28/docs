A weya webhook sends the result of every finished call in a campaign to your endpoint as a JSON `POST` request. Each result carries the call status, recording, the values of your post-call metrics and the contact's own data, so you can update your CRM, trigger a follow-up or feed a report without polling.

## Set up a webhook

A webhook is a URL registered once for your organization and then attached to the campaigns that should use it. Each organization can have up to 5 webhooks.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the webhook">
    Register your endpoint URL in the dashboard, or with [Create Webhook](/api-reference/Webhooks/createWebhooks):

    ```bash
    curl --location 'https://api.weya.live/backend/api/webhooks' \
      --header "x-api-key: $WEYA_API_KEY" \
      --header 'Content-Type: application/json' \
      --data '{
        "name": "Call results",
        "url": "https://your-domain.com/weya/call-results",
        "bearerToken": "YOUR_ENDPOINT_TOKEN"
      }'
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach it to a campaign">
    In the dashboard, add the webhook to the campaign under **Track Call Outcomes**. Through the API, set `pushingCrms` when you [upload a campaign](/api-reference/Campaigns/uploadCSV) or [update one](/api-reference/Campaigns/updateBatch):

    ```json
    "pushingCrms": [{ "type": "webhook", "crmId": "<webhook _id>", "schemaId": "<webhook _id>" }]
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return 2xx from your endpoint">
    Respond with any `2xx` status within 10 seconds. Do slow work, such as CRM writes, after you respond.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  A webhook attached to a campaign receives only calls that end **after** it was attached. To send calls that already finished, select them in the dashboard and use the bulk CRM sync.
</Warning>

## Securing your endpoint

- If you set `bearerToken`, every delivery includes `Authorization: Bearer <token>`. Reject requests without it.
- Deliveries come from a fixed weya IP address. If your firewall only accepts known addresses, ask weya for it.
- Only the URL and bearer token are used. Custom headers and body templates are not sent.
- Register the final URL of your endpoint. weya follows redirects, but a `301` or `302` turns the `POST` into a `GET`, so the body is lost.

<ResponseField name="phoneNumber" type="string">
  The contact's phone number.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="callingNumber" type="string">
  The weya phone number the call was made from or received on.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="call_direction" type="string">
  Whether the call was outbound or inbound.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="callStatus" type="string">
  How the attempt ended. See [call statuses](/get-started/concepts#call-statuses).
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="call_outcome" type="string">
  The call's outcome from the post-call analysis. Empty when no outcome was determined.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="call_started_at" type="string">
  When the call started (ISO 8601), in the campaign's timezone.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="duration" type="number">
  Length of the call.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="recordingUrl" type="string">
  Link to the call recording, when there is one.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="call_analysis" type="array[object]">
  One entry per [post-call metric](/single-prompt/step4) defined on the agent, each with `metric_name` and `metric_value`.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="lead_data" type="object">
  The contact's variables, as uploaded: the columns of your CSV or the fields you sent in `callData`.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="next_attempt_after" type="string">
  When the next attempt to this contact is scheduled, in the campaign's timezone. Empty when no further attempt is scheduled.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="is_expired" type="boolean">
  `true` when weya will make no further attempts to this contact. `expired_reason` says why.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="initial_lead_data" type="object" deprecated>
  No longer populated. Use `lead_data`.
</ResponseField>

## When results are sent

weya sends one result per finished attempt with one of these statuses: `completed`, `no-answer`, `busy`, `failed`, `voicemail`, `ivr`, `canceled`, `timeout` or `aborted`. Attempts that were never dialled don't produce a webhook.

The first delivery happens about **five minutes after the call ends**, so the recording and the transcript analysis are included. If the analysis still isn't ready, weya checks again every three minutes for up to 30 minutes.

<Warning>
  A `completed` call whose analysis still isn't ready after 30 minutes is not sent. Watch for completed calls that never reach your endpoint, and check that the agent has post-call metrics defined.
</Warning>

## Retries

If your endpoint doesn't return `2xx`, weya decides whether to try again from the response:

| Your endpoint returns | What weya does |
| --- | --- |
| `2xx` | Delivered. No more attempts |
| No response within 10 seconds, a network error, `408`, `429` or `5xx` | Retries, up to 8 deliveries in total over about 22 hours |
| Any other `4xx` | Stops. The result is not sent again |

Retries wait 1 minute, 5 minutes, 15 minutes, 1 hour, 3 hours, 6 hours, then 12 hours, each with up to 10% random jitter. If you return a `Retry-After` header, weya waits that long instead, between 30 seconds and 6 hours. Pick a response below to see the schedule.

Retry schedule for network errors, timeouts, 408, 429 and 5xx: delivery 1 at about 5 minutes after the call ends, then retries after 1 minute, 5 minutes, 15 minutes, 1 hour, 3 hours, 6 hours and 12 hours (8 deliveries, about 22 hours of retries). Other 4xx responses stop delivery immediately.

## Handling duplicates

Your endpoint can receive the same attempt more than once, for example when the recording arrives after the first delivery and weya re-sends the result with it. Treat `request_uuid` as the key: store the latest payload for each `request_uuid` and overwrite older ones.

<Tip>
  To build one record per contact, group results by `call_id` and keep the one with the latest `call_started_at`.
</Tip>

## Stop sending results

Remove the webhook from the campaign's **Track Call Outcomes**, or delete the webhook to stop it in every campaign at once. Retries that are already scheduled may still arrive.

export const RetrySchedule = () => {
  const waits = [1, 5, 15, 60, 180, 360, 720];
  const options = [
    { id: "5xx", label: "5xx, 408, 429 or timeout", retry: true },
    { id: "2xx", label: "2xx", retry: false, ok: true },
    { id: "4xx", label: "Other 4xx (400, 401, 404...)", retry: false },
  ];
  const [choice, setChoice] = useState("5xx");
  const opt = options.find((o) => o.id === choice);
  const fmt = (m) => (m < 60 ? m + " min" : (m / 60 % 1 === 0 ? m / 60 : (m / 60).toFixed(1)) + " h");
  let total = 0;
  const rows = [{ n: 1, wait: null, at: 0 }];
  if (opt.retry) {
    waits.forEach((w, i) => { total += w; rows.push({ n: i + 2, wait: w, at: total }); });
  }
  return (
    <div className="not-prose" style={{ border: "1px solid rgba(127,127,127,.25)", borderRadius: 12, padding: 16, display: "grid", gap: 12 }}>
      <div style={{ display: "flex", flexWrap: "wrap", gap: 8 }}>
        {options.map((o) => (
          <button key={o.id} type="button" aria-pressed={choice === o.id} onClick={() => setChoice(o.id)}
            style={{ padding: "6px 12px", borderRadius: 8, font: "inherit", fontSize: 13, color: "inherit", cursor: "pointer",
              border: choice === o.id ? "2px solid #4F1AD6" : "1px solid rgba(127,127,127,.35)",
              background: choice === o.id ? "rgba(79,26,214,.12)" : "transparent" }}>
            Endpoint returns {o.label}
          </button>
        ))}
      </div>
      <div style={{ display: "grid", gap: 6 }}>
        {rows.map((r) => (
          <div key={r.n} style={{ display: "flex", alignItems: "center", gap: 8, fontSize: 13, minWidth: 0 }}>
            <span style={{ width: 84, flexShrink: 0 }}>Delivery {r.n}</span>
            <div style={{ flex: 1, minWidth: 0, height: 8, borderRadius: 4, background: "rgba(127,127,127,.18)", position: "relative" }}>
              <div style={{ position: "absolute", left: (r.at / 1341) * 100 + "%", top: -2, width: 12, height: 12, marginLeft: -6, borderRadius: 6, background: "#4F1AD6" }} />
            </div>
            <span style={{ width: 150, flexShrink: 0, textAlign: "right", opacity: 0.8 }}>
              {r.wait === null ? "~5 min after call" : "+" + fmt(r.wait) + " (at " + fmt(r.at) + ")"}
            </span>
          </div>
        ))}
      </div>
      <div style={{ fontSize: 13, opacity: 0.8 }}>
        {opt.ok ? "Delivered on the first try. weya sends nothing more for this attempt." : opt.retry ? "8 deliveries in total, the last about " + fmt(total) + " after the first. If all fail, weya gives up on this result." : "weya stops after the first delivery. Fix the endpoint; this result will not be retried."}
      </div>
    </div>
  );
};
